## Overall finding

The current prototype contains valuable foundations and should not be discarded. Its strongest aligned pieces are the visual language, real voice interaction, Microsoft proof of capability, deterministic calendar helpers, durable onboarding/profile records, early identity primitives, model-usage records, meeting knowledge, and testable utility modules.

The central gap is not missing feature volume. It is that the prototype still behaves like a capable foreground assistant application, while Canon requires a trusted, governed, event-driven coworker platform.
